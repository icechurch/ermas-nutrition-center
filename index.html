Our story

A Bay Area tradition of healthy living

Erma’s Nutrition Center and Natural Market brings together supplements, specialty groceries and a personal approach to shopping. Our roots in the Houston Bay Area reach back to 1967, and we’ve been proudly serving our neighbors ever since.

Today you’ll find us on NASA Parkway in Nassau Bay, with shelves full of vitamins, organic produce, natural groceries and specialty foods, and a team that’s always glad to help you find something for your everyday routine.

We’re also proud to host the Nassau Bay Farmers Market, connecting local growers and makers directly with our community every Saturday.

Nassau Bay Farmers Market
